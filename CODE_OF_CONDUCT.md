# Code of Conduct

This project abides by the [Contributor Covenant, v2.0][covenant].

## In short

Interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and
healthy community. Be respectful of differing opinions and experiences, give and
accept constructive feedback gracefully, and take responsibility for mistakes.

Harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks,
publishing others' private information, and other conduct that would reasonably
be considered inappropriate are not acceptable.

## Scope

This applies within all project spaces, and when an individual is representing
the project in public spaces.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to **hello@resnovas.com**. All complaints are
reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy and
security are respected.

The full text, including the enforcement guidelines, is at the link above.

[covenant]: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/0/code_of_conduct/
